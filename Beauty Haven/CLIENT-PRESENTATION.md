
# BEAUTY HAVEN — Nail Salon Website Demo

## Website features

- Premium salon homepage
- Beauty and nail services with sample prices
- Nail design photo gallery
- Appointment request form
- WhatsApp booking integration
- Customer reviews section
- Mobile-friendly navigation
- Responsive desktop and mobile design

## Three-minute client demonstration

### 1. Homepage
Show the salon branding, headline, photographs and booking buttons.

### 2. Services
Explain how customers can view treatments, descriptions and prices.

### 3. Gallery
Show how the salon can display its own nail designs and attract customers.

### 4. WhatsApp booking
Choose a service, complete the appointment form and demonstrate how the booking details open in WhatsApp.

Do not send the demonstration booking message.

### 5. Mobile experience
Show the website on a simulated phone screen.

## What can be customized for a salon?

- Salon name and logo
- Brand colours
- Services and prices
- Salon photographs
- WhatsApp number
- Contact information
- Opening hours
- Address and Google Maps
- Social media accounts
- Genuine customer reviews

## Important information

This is a demonstration website.

Prices, photographs and testimonials are examples and must be replaced with approved salon content before launch.

WhatsApp bookings are appointment requests, not automatic confirmations. The salon must confirm availability manually.

The current version does not have a database or online payment system.
